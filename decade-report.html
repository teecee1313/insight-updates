📋 Decade Signal Report — every key, ten years, the real engine

Reads the stored decade grading (4 independent quarters of the liquid universe · hold 10 sessions · edge measured vs each quarter's own market · fires = signal-day events). Enter the admin key; nothing is stored.
Reading it: fires = times the key fired across ten years (all quarters summed) · up% = raw share of fires that rose · beat-mkt% = fires that beat their own day's market (the honest win rate) · edge/fire = average excess vs the market per fire, in % (≈ dollars per $100 before costs; brokerage on $500 ≈ 1.2%) · t = weakest quarter's t-statistic (strength of evidence) · Q = quarters in which the key had ≥30 fires. PROVEN·10Y = pooled t ≥ 2.5 AND edge above round-trip costs (1.2%) — the decade's honour roll, same standard the product uses. SOLID·10Y = pooled t ≥ 2.0. CONSISTENT = positive in every quarter but below the t bar. MIXED = positive overall. FAILED = non-positive edge. Research, not advice.
